Your favorite photo becomes a paint-by-number template, and you paint it with Apple Pencil. Real painting instead of tapping fields.

YOUR PHOTO AS A TEMPLATE
Pick a photo and the app turns it into fields with fine black outlines and numbers. Two sliders set how many colors there are and how fine the fields get. The preview shows you the result right away.

PAINT LIKE WITH A BRUSH
You paint stroke by stroke with Apple Pencil, freely and precisely, just like on paper. Nothing fills itself in, and how clean every edge turns out is entirely up to you.

AT YOUR OWN PACE
No timer, no scores, no notifications. Use your fingers to move, zoom and rotate the picture like a sheet of paper. Finished fields fill in cleanly, finished colors get a check mark.

YOUR PICTURE STAYS
Every stroke is saved automatically. When the picture is done, hide the outlines and share it.

ALSO
• Over 70 motifs in 10 themed packs to start right away
• 4 to 30 colors, from coarse to very fine
• Three brush sizes, undo also with a double-tap on Apple Pencil
• No pencil? Paint with your finger
• Light and dark mode, VoiceOver support
• Works fully offline

COMPLETELY FREE
The motifs and as many of your own photos as you like: everything is free. No in-app purchases, no subscription.

No ads, no account, no data collection. Your photos never leave your iPad.
